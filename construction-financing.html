Development capital

Construction lenders size risk before they size proceeds.

Construction financing is reviewed through loan-to-cost, as-complete loan-to-value, project budget, contingency, borrower equity, sponsor track record and exit strategy.

Draws are typically released as work progresses, subject to inspection and lender conditions. A strong submission explains not only what will be built, but how the project will be completed, stabilized, sold or refinanced.

Prepare early

  • Detailed development budget
  • Plans, permits and zoning status
  • Borrower equity and source of funds
  • Appraisal and environmental reports
  • Pre-leasing, pre-sales or takeout plan
Sizing tests

Two common constraints guide construction loan proceeds.

01

Loan-to-cost

Compares the loan to total project cost, including land, hard costs, soft costs, financing costs and contingency.

02

As-complete value

Compares the loan to the appraiser's projected value once the project is complete.

03

Takeout strategy

Shows how the construction facility will be repaid through sale, refinance or permanent term debt.
