Refinance strategy

Use the refinance event to improve the whole capital stack.

A commercial refinance can solve more than an upcoming maturity. It can release equity, reset amortization, improve covenants, consolidate debt or support a new acquisition.

Lenders review current value, net operating income, lease stability, existing mortgage terms, borrower financials and the use of proceeds. If the property has changed since the original loan, the refinance should show that improvement clearly.

Common objectives

  • Upcoming mortgage maturity
  • Equity take-out for business or acquisition plans
  • Debt consolidation and payment restructuring
  • Replacing bridge or private capital
  • Improving term, amortization or reporting requirements
Key review points

What affects refinance proceeds.

01

Updated value

Current appraised value and market evidence drive lender loan-to-value calculations.

02

Cash flow

Net operating income and debt service coverage often limit proceeds before value does.

03

Use of funds

Lenders want to understand whether funds are for growth, consolidation, reserves or stabilization.
