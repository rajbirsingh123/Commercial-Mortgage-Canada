Development sites

Land financing depends on the path from dirt to exit.

Land and development files are reviewed through zoning, approvals, servicing, market demand, borrower equity, sponsor experience and the credibility of the next financing event.

Because raw land and early-stage development carry more execution risk, lender fit can vary widely. A strong package explains the project stage, budget, timeline and realistic repayment source.

Important details

  • Current zoning and approval status
  • Servicing and infrastructure plan
  • Environmental and geotechnical status
  • Project budget and equity invested
  • Sale, refinance or construction takeout strategy
