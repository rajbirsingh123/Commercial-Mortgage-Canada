Industrial assets

Durable demand, specialized underwriting.

Industrial properties are reviewed through location, clear heights, loading, truck access, tenancy, environmental history, lease terms and market demand.

Owner-occupied industrial financing also considers the operating business, profitability, cash flow and borrower balance sheet.

Lender focus

  • Tenant covenant and lease maturity
  • Building functionality and access
  • Environmental and use considerations
  • NOI, DSCR and vacancy assumptions
  • Owner-user business income where applicable
