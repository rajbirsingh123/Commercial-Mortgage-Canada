Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through Commercial Mortgage Canada may be collected and used.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, contact details, property information, financing requirements and other information you provide through our forms or correspondence.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to your inquiry, assess a potential financing request, communicate with you, arrange review by appropriate mortgage professionals and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing information

Where you request financing assistance, relevant information may be shared with lenders, brokers, appraisers or other service providers as reasonably required to evaluate or complete the transaction. We do not sell personal information.

Consent and contact

By submitting a form, you consent to being contacted about your request by telephone, email or text message. You may withdraw consent or ask about the information we hold by contacting us through our contact page.

Updates

This policy may be updated as our services or legal requirements change. Please review it periodically.
