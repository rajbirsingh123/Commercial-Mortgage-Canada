Retail and office

Lease quality and market position matter.

Retail and office loans are shaped by tenant mix, lease expiry schedule, vacancy, location strength, building condition and the borrower's plan for the property.

For owner-occupied buildings, the operating business is reviewed alongside the real estate so the financing structure reflects both the property and the company behind it.
